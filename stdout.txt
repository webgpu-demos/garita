== citation

== purpose
"Puerto Rico regularly experiences rainfall-induced landslides, with intense rainfall often triggering widespread landsliding. To improve the ability to reduce hazards to people and the built environment posed by rainfall-induced landslides, we developed a map depicting an estimate of the relative susceptibility to such landslides."
